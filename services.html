Four kinds of work, one rate of $95 an hour, and a quote that's the hours times the rate. Each service says who it suits and who it doesn't.

Power BI · DAX · Looker · Excel · SQL

Dashboards and reporting

One trusted place to see the numbers you already argue about. Power BI, Looker or Excel, built on your data and checked against it, with a short guide so your team can keep it running.

  • A data model you can read, with each measure written down
  • Row-level security when different people should see different things
  • Scheduled refresh, with a warning when it fails
  • A handover session, recorded

Typical package: Dashboard sprint, 25 h × $95 = $2,375

● A good fit

You have the data somewhere, in spreadsheets, a POS, a CRM or a database, and nobody trusts the totals.

Not a fit

You need a large team on site next week. JA Insights is one senior analyst, and you'll know that before you sign.

Python · R · regression · forecasting · experiment design

Analysis and forecasting

A straight answer to a specific question, with the uncertainty stated. Pricing, demand, churn, staffing, valuation. A written answer, the code that produced it, and how sure the answer is.

  • A one-page answer first, with the detail behind it
  • Ranges, not single numbers pretending to be certain
  • Code you can rerun next quarter
  • A plain list of what would change the answer

Typical package: Focused analysis, 20 h × $95 = $1,900

● A good fit

You have a decision coming and want evidence, not a deck.

Not a fit

The answer has already been chosen and needs a chart to support it.

Python · SQL · Postgres · Power Automate · scheduled jobs

Automation and data cleanup

The report someone rebuilds by hand every Monday, built once. Pipelines that pull, clean and check your data on a schedule, so the copy-and-paste work stops and the errors stop with it.

  • Imports from the tools you already use
  • Checks that flag duplicates, gaps and impossible values
  • A log of every run
  • Documentation a successor can follow

Typical package: Report automation, 16 h × $95 = $1,520

● A good fit

Someone on your team spends hours a week moving numbers between files.

Not a fit

You need a full software product. That's a bigger build, and it's quoted as one.
