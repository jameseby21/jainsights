Approach

How the work is done, and how it's priced.

Six rules that don't change from project to project, the process, and the rate next to what the market charges.

Show the source

Every figure in a report links back to where it came from. If a number can't be traced, it doesn't ship.

Price the hours

One rate, published. A quote is the hours times the rate, and you can see both.

Say how sure

Forecasts come with ranges. Some answers are “we can't tell yet,” and you'll hear that early.

You own it

The code, the data model and the documents are yours. No lock-in, no licence back to me.

Small on purpose

One senior analyst, so you always talk to the person doing the work.

Open by default

Most tools behind this work are free and public at jebyLabs. You pay for time, care and hosting.

Nothing is billed before you approve a written quote.

Questions people ask

Straight answers. If yours isn't here, ask it on the call.

Is it just you?

Yes. JA Insights is James Eby. If a project needs more hands, you'll hear that before any quote, along with who else would be involved.

Why publish the rate?

So you can compare before we talk. The rate sits between freelance generalists and regional consultancies; the comparison and its source are on this page.

What if the work takes longer than quoted?

The quote holds. If the scope changes, you get a written change with the new hours before any extra work starts.

How do I pay?

Projects: half to start, half on handover. Care plans: monthly, cancel any time. Card or bank transfer.

Who owns what you build?

You do: code, data model, documentation and dashboards. I keep only general know-how and the open-source tools I already published.

Do you sign an NDA?

Yes, before you share anything sensitive. Client data stays in your systems whenever that's possible.

Do you work outside Miami?

Yes. Most work is remote. On-site visits in South Florida are included; travel elsewhere is billed at cost.
